# T10 — Thermal Comfort Index Model (PMV/PPD)

> **Status: specified** (owner review 2026-10-23; drafted in KG sprint 1). Filled to the ML-KG-04 fields (MLM SPECS2 §12). Closes SG-T10-1 (mlm-library SPEC_GAPS.md). Owner decisions are recorded in REVIEW.md.

## Purpose
Computes the Predicted Mean Vote (PMV, the mean thermal sensation vote of a large group on the seven-point scale −3 cold … +3 hot) and the Predicted Percentage of Dissatisfied (PPD, %) for an occupied zone from the six Fanger variables: air temperature, mean radiant temperature, relative air speed, humidity, metabolic rate and clothing insulation. It turns the T01 indoor temperature into a comfort index that [[O03]] uses to trigger occupant overrides and that comfort KPIs ([[T18]]) report.

## Layer
**Deterministic** — a pure function of its inputs and parameters (ML-LIB-02). The only iteration is the fixed-point solution for clothing surface temperature, which converges deterministically.

---

## Formula

### LaTeX (Fanger 1970, as standardised in ISO 7730)
$$PMV = \left(0.303\, e^{-0.036 M} + 0.028\right) L$$

with the thermal load $L$ (W/m²), the difference between internal heat production and heat loss of a person at comfort-level skin temperature and sweat rate:
$$
\begin{aligned}
L ={} & (M - W) - 3.05\times10^{-3}\left[5733 - 6.99(M - W) - p_a\right] - 0.42\left[(M - W) - 58.15\right] \\
& - 1.7\times10^{-5} M (5867 - p_a) - 0.0014\, M (34 - t_a) \\
& - 3.96\times10^{-8} f_{cl}\left[(t_{cl} + 273)^4 - (\bar{t}_r + 273)^4\right] - f_{cl}\, h_c\, (t_{cl} - t_a)
\end{aligned}
$$
(the sweat term $0.42[(M-W) - 58.15]$ is set to 0 when $M - W \le 58.15$ W/m²), where
$$t_{cl} = 35.7 - 0.028(M - W) - I_{cl}\left\{3.96\times10^{-8} f_{cl}\left[(t_{cl}+273)^4 - (\bar{t}_r+273)^4\right] + f_{cl} h_c (t_{cl} - t_a)\right\}$$
$$h_c = \max\left(2.38\,|t_{cl} - t_a|^{0.25},\; 12.1\sqrt{v_{ar}}\right), \qquad f_{cl} = \begin{cases} 1.00 + 1.290\, I_{cl} & I_{cl} \le 0.078 \\ 1.05 + 0.645\, I_{cl} & I_{cl} > 0.078 \end{cases}$$
$$p_a = \frac{RH}{100} \cdot 1000\, e^{\,16.6536 - 4030.183/(t_a + 235)} \;\;[\text{Pa}], \qquad M = 58.15\cdot \text{met}, \quad I_{cl} = 0.155\cdot \text{clo}$$
$$PPD = 100 - 95\, e^{-0.03353\, PMV^4 - 0.2179\, PMV^2} \;\;[\%]$$

### Description
PMV is Fanger's heat-balance index: the thermal load L is what the body would have to store or shed if skin temperature and sweat rate stayed at their comfort values, and the factor $0.303 e^{-0.036M} + 0.028$ converts that load into a mean vote. Clothing surface temperature $t_{cl}$ appears on both sides of its own equation (radiation and convection from the clothing depend on it), so it is found by fixed-point iteration. PPD maps PMV to the expected share of people dissatisfied; it has a minimum of 5 % at PMV = 0, because even at neutral some people are dissatisfied. The previous note gave only the first line, without L, M, clothing or PPD (SG-T10-1).

### Python Pseudocode (reference algorithm, ISO 7730 Annex D)
```python
import math

def pmv_ppd(t_a, t_r, v_ar, rh, met=1.2, clo=1.0, wme=0.0):
    """T10. t_a, t_r in degC; v_ar relative air speed m/s; rh in %; met, clo, wme in met/clo/met."""
    p_a = rh * 10 * math.exp(16.6536 - 4030.183 / (t_a + 235))   # water vapour pressure, Pa
    icl = 0.155 * clo                    # m2K/W
    m, w = met * 58.15, wme * 58.15      # W/m2
    mw = m - w
    fcl = 1.0 + 1.29 * icl if icl <= 0.078 else 1.05 + 0.645 * icl
    hcf = 12.1 * math.sqrt(v_ar)         # forced convection
    taa, tra = t_a + 273, t_r + 273
    # fixed-point iteration for clothing surface temperature (scaled by 1/100)
    tcla = taa + (35.5 - t_a) / (3.5 * icl + 0.1)
    p1 = icl * fcl; p2 = p1 * 3.96; p3 = p1 * 100; p4 = p1 * taa
    p5 = 308.7 - 0.028 * mw + p2 * (tra / 100) ** 4
    xn, xf = tcla / 100, tcla / 50
    for _ in range(150):
        if abs(xn - xf) <= 0.00015:
            break
        xf = (xf + xn) / 2
        hc = max(hcf, 2.38 * abs(100 * xf - taa) ** 0.25)
        xn = (p5 + p4 * hc - p2 * xf ** 4) / (100 + p3 * hc)
    else:
        raise RuntimeError("t_cl iteration did not converge")
    tcl = 100 * xn - 273
    L = (mw
         - 3.05e-3 * (5733 - 6.99 * mw - p_a)               # skin diffusion
         - (0.42 * (mw - 58.15) if mw > 58.15 else 0.0)     # sweating
         - 1.7e-5 * m * (5867 - p_a)                         # latent respiration
         - 0.0014 * m * (34 - t_a)                           # dry respiration
         - 3.96 * fcl * (xn ** 4 - (tra / 100) ** 4)         # radiation
         - fcl * hc * (tcl - t_a))                           # convection
    pmv = (0.303 * math.exp(-0.036 * m) + 0.028) * L
    ppd = 100 - 95 * math.exp(-0.03353 * pmv ** 4 - 0.2179 * pmv ** 2)
    return pmv, ppd
```

### Plain English
Work out how much heat a person makes (activity) and how much they lose through their skin, breath, sweat, and by radiation and convection from their clothes to the room. If they lose exactly what they make, they feel neutral (PMV = 0). Too little loss and PMV goes positive (warm); too much and it goes negative (cool). PPD turns that into the percentage of people who would be unhappy.

---

## Method
Fanger's PMV/PPD model (Fanger 1970), in the form standardised by ISO 7730 (Ergonomics of the thermal environment — analytical determination and interpretation of thermal comfort using calculation of the PMV and PPD indices and local thermal comfort criteria). The algorithm above is the computer program of ISO 7730:2005 Annex D. The open-source reference implementation `pythermalcomfort` (Tartarini & Schiavon 2020) states that the 2005 and 2025 editions use identical PMV/PPD equations and applicability limits; the test vectors below agree with it to within 2×10⁻⁴ in PMV. ASHRAE 55 uses the same core PMV model.

## RD Role
Deterministic layer, RD lifecycle step 1 (bounds). T10 does not estimate an RD. It defines the comfort side of the feasible domain: an action whose predicted T_in gives |PMV| above the category limit is outside the comfort bound that [[O03]] and [[C10]] enforce. Its local sensitivity ∂PMV/∂T_in (≈ 0.22 per K at the default conditions, TV-T10-14) converts a thermal RD (e.g. ∂T_in/∂activation from T01/C01) into a comfort RD, ∂PMV/∂activation = (∂PMV/∂T_in)(∂T_in/∂activation), which is how comfort limits the usable Flex↑ of heat-pump assets.

## Validity Limits
- Inputs (ISO 7730 clause 4): 10 < t_a < 30 °C, 10 < t_r < 40 °C, 0 < v_ar < 1 m/s, 0.8 < met < 4, 0 < clo < 2, 0 < p_a < 2700 Pa; PMV only between −2 and +2. Outside these, return the value with `valid = false`; consumers must not act on it.
- Steady state: PMV assumes people in thermal equilibrium with a steady environment; it is not valid for transients (e.g. the first minutes after an override or a setback recovery).
- Population average: PMV predicts a group mean; individual votes scatter widely around it. Adaptive comfort (naturally ventilated, free-running buildings) is not covered by this model.
- Uniform environment: local discomfort (draught, radiant asymmetry, vertical gradient) is outside T10.
