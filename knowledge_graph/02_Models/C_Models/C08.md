# C08 — Response Derivative Engine

> **Status: specified** (owner review 2026-10-23; drafted in KG sprint 1). Filled to the ML-KG-04 fields (MLM SPECS2 §12). Closes SG-C08-4 (synthesis rule) and SG-C08-5 (units); proposes a rule for SG-C08-7 (bounds). SG-C08-3, SG-C08-6 and SG-C08-8 stay open (see Gap closure). Owner decisions are recorded in REVIEW.md.

## Purpose
Produces the Response Derivative (RD) of flexibility with respect to each stimulus — how much upward (or downward) flexibility changes per unit change in PV, state of charge, outdoor or indoor temperature, price or activation signal — for every asset, building or fragment, as a structured object with an expected value, uncertainty, bounds, route and validity (RD lifecycle step 4, "synthesis"). It is the decision primitive that [[C10]] governs, [[C05]] and [[C09]] use for action evaluation, and [[P07]] aggregates.

## Layer
**RL/Q-Learning** — RD synthesis step of the decision layer (Bible §25, step 4). C08 does not choose actions; RD is never a reward (ML-RD-06, FX-RD-05).

## Formula

### LaTeX
**Definition** (unchanged):
$$RD_i(t) = \frac{\partial\, \text{Flex}^{\uparrow}(t)}{\partial x_i(t)}$$
(and the same for Flex↓), in units of W per unit of $x_i$.

**Route 1, LP dual** (`lp_dual`, model-based, semantics `schedule_held`): from the [[C01]] stage-2 flexibility LP, with $x_i$ entered as the equality $x_i = \bar{x}_i$,
$$\widehat{RD}_i = \lambda^*_i, \quad \text{signed so that} \quad \lambda^*_i = \lim_{h\to 0} \frac{\text{Flex}^*(\bar{x}_i + h) - \text{Flex}^*(\bar{x}_i)}{h}$$

**Route 2, finite difference on the model** (`finite_difference_model`, semantics `total` when C01 re-dispatches, `schedule_held` when the schedule is held):
$$\widehat{RD}_i = \frac{\text{Flex}(\bar{x}_i + h) - \text{Flex}(\bar{x}_i - h)}{2h}, \qquad s_i = \left|D(h) - D(h/2)\right|$$

**Routes 3 and 4, observed** (`finite_difference_observed`, `regression`; PARA//EL asset level): per-event ratios $\Delta R / \Delta X$ (mean, population s.d.), or the OLS slope of $R$ on $X$ with its standard error (library D-14).

**Synthesis across routes** (SG-C08-4): for the $n$ estimates $(\hat\theta_r, s_r)$ that share stimulus, response, direction, scope, validity window and semantic class, random-effects inverse-variance pooling:
$$w_r = \frac{1}{s_r^2}, \quad \hat\theta_{FE} = \frac{\sum_r w_r \hat\theta_r}{\sum_r w_r}, \quad Q = \sum_r w_r (\hat\theta_r - \hat\theta_{FE})^2$$
$$\hat\tau^2 = \max\!\left(0,\; \frac{Q - (n-1)}{\sum_r w_r - \sum_r w_r^2 / \sum_r w_r}\right), \quad w^*_r = \frac{1}{s_r^2 + \hat\tau^2}$$
$$RD = \frac{\sum_r w^*_r \hat\theta_r}{\sum_r w^*_r}, \qquad SE(RD) = \left(\sum_r w^*_r\right)^{-1/2}, \qquad I^2 = \max\!\left(0,\; \frac{Q - (n-1)}{Q}\right)$$
$$q_{10}, q_{90} = RD \mp 1.28155\; SE(RD)$$
with $s_r$ floored at $10^{-6} \max(1, |\hat\theta_r|)$ so that a point estimate (an LP dual from one scenario, $s = 0$) has a finite weight. With $n = 1$ the route estimate passes through unchanged.

**Bounds** (SG-C08-7, FX-RD-03): with deterministic bounds $[L_i, U_i]$ for $RD_i$,
$$RD^{final}_i = \begin{cases} RD_i & L_i \le RD_i \le U_i \quad (\texttt{within}) \\ \text{nearest bound} & \text{outside, but within } k_{SE}\cdot SE(RD_i) \text{ of it} \quad (\texttt{clipped}) \\ \text{none} & \text{otherwise} \quad (\texttt{invalidated}) \end{cases}$$

### Description
C08 collects every estimate of the same derivative, from the C01 LP duals, from finite differences on the model, and from observed responses, and turns them into one RD with uncertainty. Estimates are only combined when they measure the same thing: the LP dual holds the dispatch schedule fixed (`schedule_held`) while a re-dispatching finite difference or an observed response includes the schedule's reaction (`total`); library D-11 shows the two can differ in sign, so they are reported separately and never averaged. Within a class, more precise estimates get more weight (inverse variance), and when the routes disagree by more than their own uncertainties explain, the between-route variance τ² widens the result and lowers the weight differences (random effects). I² (0–1) is the share of the spread due to disagreement between routes; it is reported as the route-incoherence of the RD. Finally the RD is checked against the bounds the deterministic layer gives: slightly outside is clipped and recorded, far outside is invalidated, because RD must stay physically meaningful (Bible §24.2). The previous note gave only the LP-dual definition; the Bible's step 4, "combine sensitivities into unified RD with uncertainty", had no rule (SG-C08-4).

### Python Pseudocode
```python
import numpy as np

CLASSES = {'lp_dual': 'schedule_held', 'finite_difference_model': None,   # from the estimate
           'finite_difference_observed': 'total', 'regression': 'total', 'rd_fusion': 'total'}

def synthesise(estimates, k_se=2.0, rel_floor=1e-6):
    """estimates: list of dict(theta, s, route, semantics, key=(stimulus, response, direction,
    scope, validity)). Returns one RD per (key, semantic class)."""
    out = {}
    for key_class, group in group_by(estimates, lambda e: (e['key'],
                                     e['semantics'] or CLASSES[e['route']])).items():
        th = np.array([e['theta'] for e in group]); s = np.array([e['s'] for e in group])
        if len(th) == 1:
            rd, se, tau2, I2 = th[0], s[0], 0.0, 0.0
        else:
            s = np.maximum(s, rel_floor * np.maximum(1.0, np.abs(th)))
            w = 1 / s**2
            fe = (w * th).sum() / w.sum()
            Q = (w * (th - fe)**2).sum()
            tau2 = max(0.0, (Q - (len(th) - 1)) / (w.sum() - (w**2).sum() / w.sum()))
            ws = 1 / (s**2 + tau2)
            rd, se = (ws * th).sum() / ws.sum(), ws.sum() ** -0.5
            I2 = max(0.0, (Q - (len(th) - 1)) / Q) if Q > 0 else 0.0
        out[key_class] = apply_bounds(dict(rd=rd, se=se, q10=rd - 1.28155 * se,
                                           q90=rd + 1.28155 * se, tau2=tau2, I2=I2,
                                           routes=[e['route'] for e in group]), k_se)
    return out

def apply_bounds(r, k_se):
    L, U = deterministic_bounds(r)            # from C01 / T-, G-, R-, B-series limits
    if L <= r['rd'] <= U:
        r['bounds_status'] = 'within'
    elif min(abs(r['rd'] - L), abs(r['rd'] - U)) <= k_se * r['se']:
        r['rd'] = U if r['rd'] > U else L
        r['bounds_status'] = 'clipped'
    else:
        r['rd'], r['bounds_status'] = None, 'invalidated'   # not passed to C10 as usable
    return r
```

## Method
- LP duals as sensitivities: the dual of a constraint is the derivative of the optimal value with respect to its right-hand side where that derivative exists (Boyd & Vandenberghe 2004, §5.6); duals of an LP obtained by fixing a MIP's integer variables at their optimal values (O'Neill et al. 2005), as C01 stage 2 does.
- Finite differences with a Richardson error estimate (library D-13) and observed-response estimators (library D-14).
- Synthesis: inverse-variance combination of estimates (Bates & Granger 1969, for the combination of forecasts) with the DerSimonian–Laird random-effects extension (DerSimonian & Laird 1986), Cochran's Q heterogeneity statistic (Cochran 1954) and the I² index (Higgins & Thompson 2002). The method is standard for pooling estimates of one quantity from studies (here, routes) of different precision that may disagree beyond their sampling error.
- Bounds discipline: Bible §24.2 and FLEXUS FX-RD-03 (clip, reject or invalidate, with the action recorded).

## RD Role
C08 **is** the RD synthesis step (lifecycle step 4). It receives deterministic bounds (step 1), stochastic and AI/ML sensitivities (steps 2–3, through C01 solved on C03-determinised inputs and through observed routes), and emits the RDObject that steps 5–7 consume ([[C05]], [[C09]], [[C10]], [[D08]]/F01 feedback). Field mapping to `RDObject` follows MLM SPECS2 §7.2 (stimulus, response, estimate with method and semantics, bounds with status, confidence, scope/fragment, validity, governance).

---

## Validity Limits
- An RD is local: valid at the operating point and within its validity window; for the LP route, only while C01's active constraint set does not change (at a degenerate vertex left and right derivatives differ; report both via a finite-difference check).
- Pool only estimates with the same stimulus, response, direction, scope (fragment, FX-RD-04) and semantic class; never pool `schedule_held` with `total`.
- The random-effects SE is unreliable for n = 2–3 routes (τ² is poorly estimated); I² with n ≤ 3 is indicative only.
- Pooling assumes the route errors are independent; two routes fed by the same telemetry are not independent and should be pooled first or counted once.

Never average a `schedule_held` RD with a `total` one. Essential for governance decisions in C10.
