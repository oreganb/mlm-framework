# B05 — V2G Availability Model

> **Status: specified** (owner review 2026-10-23; drafted in KG sprint 1). Filled to the ML-KG-04 fields (MLM SPECS2 §12). Closes SG-B05-1 and the B05 part of SG-LAYER-1 (mlm-library SPEC_GAPS.md). Owner decisions are recorded in REVIEW.md.

## Purpose
Decides whether an electric vehicle can discharge to the home or grid (vehicle-to-grid, V2G) now, how much power it can offer without compromising the driver's departure needs, and the probability that it will still be available over the dispatch horizon. Its flag and power limit gate any V2G dispatch in [[C01]] and multi-unit coordination in [[B07]].

## Layer
**AI/ML** — the gate itself is a deterministic rule; the AI/ML part is the EV presence forecast it consumes from [[B03]], whose probability B05 passes through as its availability probability and confidence (ML-LIB-02: AI/ML models return a ConfidenceObject). B05 trains nothing itself.

---

## Formula

### LaTeX
**Availability flag** (previous note, with the permission term defined):
$$V2G\_avail(t) = EV\_present(t) \land \left(SoC(t) > SoC^{eff}_{min}(t)\right) \land permission(t)$$
$$permission(t) = v2g\_contract \land charger\_bidirectional \land grid\_permit(t)$$

**Departure-aware SoC floor:**
$$SoC^{eff}_{min}(t) = \max\left(SoC_{V2G,min},\;\; SoC_{dep} - \frac{\eta_{ch}\, P_{chg,max}\, (t_{dep} - t)}{E_{EV}}\right)$$
(times in h, energy in Wh, power in W.)

**Available discharge power** over a dispatch duration $t_{disp}$ (h):
$$P^{V2G}_{max}(t) = \begin{cases} \min\left(P_{inv,max},\;\; \eta_{dis} \dfrac{\left(SoC(t) - SoC^{eff}_{min}(t)\right) E_{EV}}{t_{disp}}\right) & V2G\_avail(t) \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

**Availability probability** for $t+k$:
$$p^{V2G}(t+k) = P(EV\_present(t+k)) \cdot \mathbf{1}\left[SoC(t) > SoC^{eff}_{min}(t+k)\right] \cdot permission(t+k)$$

### Description
V2G is possible only if the car is plugged in, has charge to spare, and both the contract and the network allow it. "Charge to spare" now accounts for the driver: the car may only discharge down to the higher of a fixed floor (30 %) and the level from which it can still recharge to the departure target before the planned departure time. The power it can offer is the smaller of the bidirectional charger's limit (3.7 kW single-phase) and the spare energy delivered over the dispatch duration, after discharge losses. This is Kempton & Tomić's V2G power calculation, with a range buffer expressed as an SoC floor. The previous note wrote `grid_allows_V2G` in the formula but listed `V2G_contract` as the input (SG-B05-1); permission is now three explicit conditions. Because a Boolean gate cannot carry uncertainty, B05 also outputs the probability that the car will be present, taken from B03's forecast, so the AI/ML layer's confidence requirement is met without inventing one.

### Python Pseudocode
```python
def b05_v2g(EV_present, SoC, v2g_contract, charger_bidirectional, grid_permit,
            E_EV_Wh, SoC_dep, hours_to_departure, P_chg_max, p_present_forecast,
            SoC_V2G_min=0.30, eta_ch=0.90, eta_dis=0.90, P_inv_max=3700.0, t_disp_h=1.0):
    soc_floor = max(SoC_V2G_min,
                    SoC_dep - eta_ch * P_chg_max * hours_to_departure / E_EV_Wh)
    permission = v2g_contract and charger_bidirectional and grid_permit
    flag = EV_present and SoC > soc_floor and permission
    P_max = min(P_inv_max, eta_dis * (SoC - soc_floor) * E_EV_Wh / t_disp_h) if flag else 0.0
    p_avail = [p * (SoC > soc_floor) * permission for p in p_present_forecast]   # k = 1..H
    return {'V2G_avail_flag': flag, 'P_V2G_max': P_max, 'SoC_floor_eff': soc_floor,
            'p_V2G_avail': p_avail, 'b05_confidence': p_avail[0] if p_avail else float(flag)}
```

## Method
Multi-constraint Boolean gate (previous note) with the vehicle V2G power capacity of Kempton & Tomić (2005): available power is limited by the electrical connection and by stored energy less the energy reserved for driving and a range buffer, divided by the dispatch duration and multiplied by conversion efficiency. Here the reserved energy is expressed as the departure-aware SoC floor.

## RD Role
AI/ML layer, RD lifecycle step 3 and step 1 bound. B05 bounds the V2G contribution to flexibility: Flex↑ from the EV cannot exceed P_V2G_max, and ∂Flex↑/∂SoC through the EV is zero when V2G_avail is false and $\eta_{dis} E_{EV} / t_{disp}$ per unit SoC while energy-limited (TV-B05-2). p_V2G_avail weights the EV's expected contribution and is the confidence that any EV-related RD must carry.

---

## Validity Limits
- Departure time and target come from the driver; without them the floor falls back to defaults and the result should be marked as assumed.
- Linear charging model (constant P_chg,max to departure); real chargers taper above about 80 % SoC, so the floor is optimistic near full charge.
- Battery degradation cost of V2G cycling is not in B05 (belongs in C01's objective if V2G is dispatched).
- As in the C01 draft, V2G is not yet a decision variable in C01 (C01 SG-C01-6); B05's outputs are ready for when it is.

## AI Context (Cursor Alignment)
V2G is high-value flexibility but requires all three conditions plus enough spare charge. SoC_V2G_min=0.30 is a conservative safety margin — user can configure higher; the departure-aware floor can only raise it. V2G discharge rate limited to 3.7kW (single-phase). Never discharge below SoC_floor_eff: the driver's departure needs come first.
