# B01 — Battery SoC Prediction Model

> **Status: draft-for-review** (KG sprint 2, 2026-10-23). Reviewed against the ML-KG-04 fields; not `specified` until the owner has reviewed it (MLM SPECS2 §12). The physics path, efficiencies and limits are unchanged; added: the SoC-limit rule, the LSTM correction's target and state handling, method, RD role, validity limits, test vectors and references. Items marked **OWNER DECISION** are proposals; see REVIEW.md.

## Purpose
Predicts a stationary (or EV) battery's state of charge (SoC) at each time step from the charge and discharge powers dispatched by [[C01]], using Coulomb counting for the deterministic trajectory and an optional learned correction for effects the physics omits (ageing, temperature, measurement bias). Its SoC is the state that bounds storage flexibility in C01, V2G availability in [[B05]] and degradation in [[B02]].

## Layer
**AI/ML** — hybrid model. The Coulomb counting step is deterministic; the LSTM correction is learned (trained by the F-series, [[F01]]). Without a trained correction B01 is purely deterministic (library D-23).

---

## Formula

### LaTeX
**Coulomb counting (physics base)**, Δt in hours, energy in Wh, power in W:
$$E_{t+1} = E_t + \eta_{ch}\, P_{ch}[t]\, \Delta t - \frac{P_{dis}[t]}{\eta_{dis}}\, \Delta t, \qquad SoC^{phys}_{t+1} = \frac{E_{t+1}}{E_{nom}}$$

**Power limits that keep SoC within bounds** (OWNER DECISION B01-1, recommended):
$$P_{ch}^{applied} = \min\left(P_{ch},\, P_{ch,max},\, \frac{SoC_{max}E_{nom} - E_t}{\eta_{ch}\,\Delta t}\right), \qquad P_{dis}^{applied} = \min\left(P_{dis},\, P_{dis,max},\, \frac{\eta_{dis}\,(E_t - SoC_{min}E_{nom})}{\Delta t}\right)$$
and $P_{ch}\,P_{dis} = 0$ (one direction per step).

**LSTM correction** (reported SoC only):
$$SoC_{t+1} = \mathrm{clip}\left(SoC^{phys}_{t+1} + f_{LSTM}(\mathbf{x}_{hist}),\; SoC_{min},\; SoC_{max}\right)$$
where $f_{LSTM}$ is trained on the residual $SoC_{BMS} - SoC^{phys}$ and $\mathbf{x}_{hist}$ = [T_bat, P_ch, P_dis, SoC] over the last 24 steps plus cycle_count. $f_{LSTM} \equiv 0$ until a trained model exists.

### Description
Start with the energy stored. Add what was charged, times the charge efficiency (some energy is lost as heat); subtract what was discharged, divided by the discharge efficiency (more is drawn from the cells than reaches the load). Divide by capacity to get SoC. The previous note then clipped SoC to [SoC_min, SoC_max] after the step, which silently discards energy: a 4000 W charge into a nearly full battery would be reported as delivered while only 842 W could be stored (TV-B01-4). The recommended rule instead limits the power to what the battery can absorb or supply before the step and reports the power actually applied, so energy is conserved and C01, settlement and B02 see the same numbers. The learned correction adjusts the *reported* SoC; the stored-energy state carries forward from the physics, and is re-anchored to the battery management system (BMS) reading whenever one is available, so the correction cannot accumulate.

### Python Pseudocode
```python
def b01_step(E_t, P_ch, P_dis, E_nom, eta_ch=0.95, eta_dis=0.95,
             SoC_min=0.10, SoC_max=0.90, P_ch_max=float('inf'), P_dis_max=float('inf'),
             dt=900, lstm_model=None, x_hist=None, SoC_BMS=None):
    """One step. E_t in Wh; powers in W (>= 0); dt in s. Returns the next state."""
    if P_ch > 0 and P_dis > 0:
        raise ValueError("B01: charge and discharge in the same step")   # B01-3
    if SoC_BMS is not None:                    # re-anchor the state to the BMS (B01-2)
        E_t = SoC_BMS * E_nom
    dt_h = dt / 3600.0
    P_ch_a, P_dis_a = min(P_ch, P_ch_max), min(P_dis, P_dis_max)
    P_ch_a = min(P_ch_a, max(0.0, (SoC_max * E_nom - E_t) / (eta_ch * dt_h)))   # B01-1
    P_dis_a = min(P_dis_a, max(0.0, eta_dis * (E_t - SoC_min * E_nom) / dt_h))
    E_next = E_t + eta_ch * P_ch_a * dt_h - P_dis_a / eta_dis * dt_h
    SoC_phys = E_next / E_nom
    delta = lstm_model.predict(x_hist) if (lstm_model is not None and x_hist is not None) else 0.0
    SoC = min(max(SoC_phys + delta, SoC_min), SoC_max)
    return {"SoC": SoC, "E_stored_wh": E_next, "SoC_physics": SoC_phys,
            "SoC_correction": SoC - SoC_phys,
            "P_ch_applied": P_ch_a, "P_dis_applied": P_dis_a,
            "limited": (P_ch_a < min(P_ch, P_ch_max)) or (P_dis_a < min(P_dis, P_dis_max))}
```

## Method
Coulomb counting (ampere-hour integration expressed in energy), the standard SoC estimator for lithium-ion batteries, with separate charge and discharge efficiencies; its known weakness, drift from accumulated measurement error, is handled by re-anchoring to the BMS reading (Ng et al. 2009 use the same recalibration idea at full charge and rest). The correction is a long short-term memory network (Hochreiter & Schmidhuber 1997), the architecture shown by Chemali et al. (2018) to estimate Li-ion SoC from voltage, current and temperature histories; here it learns only the residual between the BMS SoC and the physics SoC, so the physics trajectory remains the model when no network is trained.

## RD Role
AI/ML layer; RD lifecycle step 1 bound and step 3 refinement. SoC is the registered stimulus `state_of_charge` and the largest RD contributor for storage. While the battery is energy-limited (below its power limit), ∂Flex↑/∂SoC = η_dis·E_nom/Δt and ∂Flex↓/∂SoC = −E_nom/(η_ch·Δt): with E_nom = 10 kWh, Δt = 0.25 h and η = 0.95, +38 000 W and −42 105 W per unit SoC (380 and −421 W/% in `rd_stimuli` units), as C01 TV-C01-4. Both are 0 when power-limited. The previous text, "every 10 % SoC ≈ E_nom × 0.10/Δt W", omitted η_dis. The power-limiting rule keeps these derivatives consistent with the energy actually available.

---

## Validity Limits
- Constant efficiencies: no dependence on power, temperature or SoC; no self-discharge; no capacity fade within the horizon (B02 handles fade).
- Usable capacity E_nom is taken as given; SoC is the fraction of usable, not nameplate, energy.
- Coulomb counting drifts without BMS re-anchoring; over long horizons with no BMS reading, the error grows with the integrated power-measurement error.
- The LSTM correction is valid only within the temperature and power range it was trained on and for the chemistry of the training battery.
- For EVs (B05), driving consumption between plug-ins is not modelled; the BMS reading at plug-in is the start state.

## AI Context
- `eta_ch` and `eta_dis` must be loaded from **DS-BATTERY by battery_id** — chemistries differ
- **SoC never leaves [SoC_min, SoC_max]**: limit the power before the step (decision B01-1) and return the applied powers; this is a physics and warranty constraint
- **P_ch and P_dis are mutually exclusive** per step; C01 enforces it with a binary, B01 refuses inputs that break it
- Re-anchor `E_t` to the BMS SoC whenever a reading is available
- The **LSTM weights** are updated by the F-series federated cycle — load the latest checkpoint from `F01.output.theta_global`; correction = 0 if none
- For ODEON deployment: battery specs come from **HotDrop meters** and **Milesight AM319** data pipeline
