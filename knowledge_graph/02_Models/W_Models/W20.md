# W20 — OKTAS-to-PV Availability Mapping Model

## Purpose
OKTAS-to-PV Availability Mapping Model in the [[W-Series]]. Layer: **Stochastic**.

## Formula

### LaTeX
$$P_{PV,avail} = G_{POA,clear} \cdot \left(1 - a\,(OKTAS/8)^{b}\right) \cdot \eta_{PV}, \qquad a = 0.75,\; b = 3.4$$

### Description
Translates OKTAS cloud score directly to available PV power fraction. Fast lookup used in real-time dispatch to estimate PV availability without full W01 computation. The cloud factor is Kasten & Czeplak (1980), the same law as W01's clear-sky mode (owner decision W01-2, 2026-10-23); it applies here because the baseline is clear-sky irradiance. Full overcast (OKTAS = 8) leaves 25 % of the clear-sky value; OKTAS = 4 leaves 92.9 %.

### Method
Empirical lookup, regression

## Weights & Parameters

- **W-W20-KCA, W-W20-KCB:** a = 0.75, b = 3.4, Kasten & Czeplak (1980), G/G_clear = 1 − 0.75 (OKTAS/8)^3.4; same as W01 clear-sky mode (W-W01-KCA/KCB, owner decision W01-2). Replaces k_cloud = 0.11, which was attributed to Perez et al. (1990), a transposition model, not a cloud law

### References
- Kasten, F., Czeplak, G. (1980). Solar and terrestrial radiation dependent on the amount and type of cloud. *Solar Energy* 24(2), 177–189. https://doi.org/10.1016/0038-092X(80)90391-6
