# W01 — Solar Irradiance Model

> **Status: specified** (KG sprint 2; owner-approved 2026-10-23, REVIEW.md). Reviewed against the ML-KG-04 fields. Owner decisions: W01-1 ρ_g = 0; W01-2 (a) cloud attenuation only in clear-sky mode, by Kasten & Czeplak (1980), replacing k_cloud = 0.11. Closes SG-W01-1 (plane-of-array transposition; `G_reflected` defined, 0 by default per OD-112) and SG-W01-2 (ensemble members are **not** clamped, per OD-113 / library D-20; [[W14]] clips its own G_POA marginal at 0).

## Purpose
Estimates the solar irradiance on the plane of a PV array or glazed surface (G_POA, W/m²) for a site and time step, from the NWP direct and diffuse irradiance and the sun's position, and gives its forecast uncertainty as an ensemble and p10/p50/p90 quantiles. Primary input to [[R01]] (PV generation), [[T04]] (solar gain for [[T01]]) and [[W14]] (joint weather scenarios).

## Layer
**Stochastic** — outputs a probability distribution over G_POA, not a point estimate. Quantiles (p10/p50/p90) are passed to the determinisation bridge [[C03]]; the members feed [[W14]] and C03's ensemble mode.

---

## Formula

### LaTeX
**Angle of incidence** on a surface of tilt β and azimuth γ (azimuths from north, 180° = south, as DS-PV), sun at zenith θ_z and azimuth γ_s:
$$\cos\theta_{AOI} = \cos\theta_z \cos\beta + \sin\theta_z \sin\beta \cos(\gamma_s - \gamma)$$

**Plane-of-array irradiance** (isotropic sky, Liu & Jordan):
$$G_{POA} = \underbrace{G_{DNI}\,\max(0, \cos\theta_{AOI})}_{G_{beam}} + \underbrace{G_{DHI}\,\frac{1+\cos\beta}{2}}_{G_{sky}} + \underbrace{\rho_g\, G_{GHI}\,\frac{1-\cos\beta}{2}}_{G_{reflected}}, \qquad G_{GHI} = G_{DNI}\cos\theta_z + G_{DHI}$$
with $G_{POA} = 0$ when $\cos\theta_z \le 0$ (sun below the horizon). Default ρ_g = 0, so $G_{reflected} = 0$ (OD-112).

**Cloud attenuation** (clear-sky input mode only; owner decision W01-2), Kasten & Czeplak (1980), with N the cloud cover in oktas:
$$G_{POA,cloud} = G_{POA} \times \left(1 - a\,(N/8)^{b}\right), \qquad a = 0.75,\; b = 3.4$$
In all-sky mode (the default; DS-NWP irradiance already includes cloud) no attenuation is applied.

**Ensemble**, members ω = 1…N, not clamped (OD-113):
$$G_{POA}^{(\omega)}[t] = G_{POA,det}[t] + \sigma_{irr}[t]\, z^{(\omega)}[t], \qquad z \sim \mathcal{N}(0,1)$$
and $p_q$ = the type-7 sample quantile (Hyndman & Fan 1996) of the members, q ∈ {0.10, 0.50, 0.90}. Members and low quantiles can be negative at low irradiance; consumers that need non-negative irradiance clip it themselves ([[W14]] clips its G_POA marginal at 0).

### Description
The old formula, $G_{DNI}\cos\theta_z + G_{DHI}$, is the irradiance on a *horizontal* surface (GHI), not on the array; it had no tilt or orientation, and $G_{reflected}$ appeared in the LaTeX but was never defined (SG-W01-1). W01 now transposes onto the array: the direct beam is scaled by the cosine of the angle between the sun and the panel's normal (zero when the sun is behind the panel), the diffuse sky is taken as uniform, so a tilted panel sees the fraction (1 + cos β)/2 of it, and the ground reflects ρ_g of the horizontal irradiance onto the fraction (1 − cos β)/2 that faces the ground. With β = 0 the formula reduces exactly to the old one (TV-W01-1). The reflected term is defined but off by default (ρ_g = 0), per OD-112. Forecast uncertainty is added as Gaussian noise with the NWP spread, as before. Members are deliberately not clamped, so W01's ensemble stays an unbiased Gaussian around the deterministic value (SG-W01-2, OD-113, library D-20); W14 clips its own G_POA marginal at 0 where non-negative scenarios are needed.

### Python Pseudocode
```python
import numpy as np

def w01_irradiance(G_DNI, G_DHI, theta_z, gamma_s, tilt, azimuth, sigma_irr,
                   rho_g=0.0, mode="all_sky", oktas=None, kc_a=0.75, kc_b=3.4,
                   n_ensemble=50, seed=None):
    """All arrays shape (T,). Angles in degrees, azimuths from north (180 = south).
    G_DNI, G_DHI, sigma_irr in W/m2. mode: 'all_sky' (NWP irradiance already includes cloud)
    or 'clear_sky' (clear-sky irradiance, attenuated by cloud cover in oktas 0-8,
    Kasten & Czeplak 1980)."""
    tz, gs = np.radians(theta_z), np.radians(gamma_s)
    b, g = np.radians(tilt), np.radians(azimuth)
    cos_z = np.cos(tz)
    cos_aoi = cos_z * np.cos(b) + np.sin(tz) * np.sin(b) * np.cos(gs - g)
    G_GHI = G_DNI * cos_z + G_DHI
    G_beam = G_DNI * np.maximum(0.0, cos_aoi)
    G_sky = G_DHI * (1 + np.cos(b)) / 2
    G_refl = rho_g * G_GHI * (1 - np.cos(b)) / 2          # 0 by default (OD-112)
    G_det = G_beam + G_sky + G_refl
    if mode == "clear_sky":
        G_det = G_det * (1.0 - kc_a * (np.asarray(oktas) / 8.0) ** kc_b)   # W01-2
    G_det = np.where(cos_z > 0, G_det, 0.0)                # sun below the horizon

    rng = np.random.Generator(np.random.PCG64(seed))     # explicit seed (library D-20)
    z = rng.standard_normal((n_ensemble, G_det.shape[0]))
    ens = G_det + sigma_irr * z                            # not clamped (SG-W01-2, OD-113)
    p10, p50, p90 = np.percentile(ens, [10, 50, 90], axis=0)   # type 7 (linear)
    return {"G_POA_det": G_det, "p10": p10, "p50": p50, "p90": p90, "ensemble": ens,
            "components": {"beam": G_beam, "sky": G_sky, "reflected": G_refl}}
```

## Method
Isotropic-sky transposition of beam and diffuse irradiance onto a tilted surface (Liu & Jordan 1963; Duffie & Beckman 2013, ch. 1–2), the standard first-order model; anisotropic models (Hay–Davies, Perez et al. 1990) are more accurate on clear days (Loutzenhiser et al. 2007) and can replace the sky term later without changing the interface. Forecast uncertainty: additive Gaussian perturbation with the NWP ensemble spread, not clamped (OD-113), so the predictive distribution is Gaussian about G_POA_det and can extend below 0 at low irradiance. Quantiles by the type-7 rule used throughout the C-series (C03).

## RD Role
Stochastic layer, RD lifecycle step 2. G_POA is a registered RD stimulus (C08-I02); W01 supplies its value and distribution, and C08's ∂Flex/∂G_POA passes through R01 (∂P_PV/∂G_POA) and C01. Within W01, ∂G_POA/∂G_DNI = max(0, cos θ_AOI) and ∂G_POA/∂G_DHI = (1 + cos β)/2, so the same NWP change moves a south-facing tilted array more than a horizontal one in winter. Members are unclamped, so a negative W01 member must not reach R01 or C01 directly: C03's ensemble mode takes members from W14, which clips G_POA at 0.

---

## Validity Limits
- Isotropic sky underestimates G_POA on clear days for tilted surfaces facing the sun (circumsolar and horizon brightening ignored); errors of several per cent are typical (Loutzenhiser et al. 2007).
- The NWP spread σ_irr is quoted for horizontal irradiance and is applied to G_POA unchanged; the Gaussian error model ignores the skew of irradiance errors. W14 and C03 inherit this.
- Members and p10 can be negative when G_POA_det is within about 1.3σ of 0 (TV-W01-9); W01 output is not physical irradiance member by member, and consumers needing non-negative values clip it (W14 does).
- No shading, soiling or horizon model; a single plane per call (one call per array orientation).
- Clear-sky mode: Kasten & Czeplak fitted the factor to global horizontal irradiance at one site (Hamburg); W01 applies the same factor to G_POA, i.e. to beam and diffuse alike. This is an approximation, and the law ignores cloud type and height.

## AI Context
When implementing W01:
- `G_DNI` and `G_DHI` come from `DS-NWP.irradiance_dni` and `DS-NWP.irradiance_dhi` — never estimated internally
- Call [[W08]] first for `theta_z` **and** `theta_az`; take `tilt_deg` and `azimuth_deg` from DS-PV (azimuth from north, 180 = south — the same convention for sun and array)
- Do not apply cloud attenuation to all-sky NWP irradiance; in clear-sky mode use Kasten & Czeplak, 1 − 0.75 (N/8)^3.4 (decision W01-2)
- Do **not** clamp members (OD-113); compute quantiles from the raw members; pass an explicit seed. Non-negative scenarios come from [[W14]], which clips G_POA at 0
- Return all outputs as a named dict/struct
