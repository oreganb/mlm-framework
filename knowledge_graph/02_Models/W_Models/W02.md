# W02 — Ambient Temperature Model

> **Status: draft-for-review** (KG sprint 1, 2026-10-23). Filled to the ML-KG-04 fields; not `specified` until the owner has reviewed it (MLM SPECS2 §12). Closes SG-W02-1 (mlm-library SPEC_GAPS.md; library D-21). Items marked **OWNER DECISION** are proposals; see REVIEW.md.

## Purpose
Produces a calibrated probabilistic forecast of outdoor air temperature at the building for each step of the horizon: median, p10/p90 and a marginal ensemble. It corrects the numerical weather prediction (NWP) forecast towards local conditions (maritime bias at coastal and island sites) and supplies T_out to [[T01]], [[R01]], [[B08]] and, through [[C03]], to [[C01]].

## Layer
**Stochastic** — returns a Gaussian predictive distribution (μ, σ), its quantiles, and a confidence (ML-LIB-02).

---

## Formula

### LaTeX
**Predictive distribution** (EMOS / MOS form):
$$T_{out}(t) \sim \mathcal{N}\left(\mu_{T,t},\, \sigma_{T,t}^2\right)$$
$$\mu_{T,t} = a + b_1\, T^{NWP}_t + b_2\, T^{hist}_t$$
$$\sigma^2_{T,t} = c + d\, S_t^2$$
where $S_t$ is the NWP ensemble spread (`temp_sigma`) and $T^{hist}_t$ is the local same-hour climatology
$$T^{hist}_t = \frac{1}{14} \sum_{n=1}^{14} T^{obs}_{t - n \cdot 24\,\text{h}}$$
**Default (untrained) coefficients:** $a = 0$, $b_1 = 0.7$, $b_2 = 0.3$; if $S_t$ is available, $c = 0, d = 1$; otherwise $d = 0$ and $c = \sigma_{default}^2$ with $\sigma_{default} = 0.8$ °C for lead ≤ 1 h and 1.5 °C beyond.
**Trained coefficients:** $(a, b_1, b_2, c, d)$ minimise the mean CRPS over the trailing 30 days, with
$$CRPS\left(\mathcal{N}(\mu,\sigma^2), y\right) = \sigma\left[z\left(2\Phi(z) - 1\right) + 2\varphi(z) - \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}}\right], \quad z = \frac{y - \mu}{\sigma}$$
**Outputs:**
$$T_{out}.q_\tau = \mu_{T,t} + \Phi^{-1}(\tau)\,\sigma_{T,t}, \quad \tau \in \{0.1, 0.5, 0.9\}; \qquad T_{out}.ens_i = \mu_{T,t} + \Phi^{-1}\!\left(\frac{i - 0.5}{N}\right)\sigma_{T,t}, \; i = 1..N$$

### Description
Outdoor temperature is forecast as a normal distribution. Its mean is the NWP forecast pulled 30 % of the way towards what the site has actually seen at the same hour over the last two weeks, which removes much of the NWP's local bias (island and coastal sites are milder than the model grid suggests). Its spread comes from the NWP ensemble when available, otherwise from a default that is tighter for the next hour than for later. Once 30 days of local observations exist, the coefficients are refitted by minimising the CRPS, the standard score for probabilistic forecasts, so the forecast becomes calibrated to the site. The marginal ensemble is a set of equally spaced quantiles (no random numbers); correlated multi-variable weather ensembles come from [[W14]]. The previous note did not say which historical value enters the blend, named ARIMA and regression without defining them, and gave no rule for choosing the nowcast or day-ahead σ (SG-W02-1).

### Python Pseudocode
```python
import numpy as np
from scipy.stats import norm

def w02_forecast(T_nwp, T_obs_history, lead_h, spread=None, coef=None, N=50):
    """T_nwp: NWP 2 m temperature for the target time (degC); T_obs_history: local hourly
    observations; lead_h: hours ahead; spread: NWP ensemble s.d. or None."""
    a, b1, b2, c, d = coef or (0.0, 0.7, 0.3, None, None)
    T_hist = np.mean([T_obs_history.at_same_hour(days_back=n) for n in range(1, 15)])
    mu = a + b1 * T_nwp + b2 * T_hist
    if c is None:                                   # untrained defaults
        if spread is not None:
            c, d = 0.0, 1.0
        else:
            c, d = (0.8 if lead_h <= 1 else 1.5) ** 2, 0.0
    sigma = np.sqrt(c + d * (spread or 0.0) ** 2)
    q = {f'p{int(t*100)}': mu + norm.ppf(t) * sigma for t in (0.1, 0.5, 0.9)}
    ens = mu + norm.ppf((np.arange(1, N + 1) - 0.5) / N) * sigma
    return {'mu': mu, 'sigma': sigma, **q, 'ensemble': ens}
```

## Method
Ensemble model output statistics (EMOS) fitted by minimum CRPS (Gneiting et al. 2005), which is a Gaussian regression of observations on NWP output in the tradition of model output statistics (Glahn & Lowry 1972); here a local same-hour climatology term is an extra predictor. The previous note's "ARIMA, ensemble, regression" is narrowed to this regression; ARIMA is not used. Quantiles and the deterministic marginal ensemble follow from the Gaussian predictive distribution (Gneiting & Katzfuss 2014).

## RD Role
Stochastic layer, RD lifecycle step 2. W02 supplies the distribution of the `weather` stimulus (°C, `registers/rd_stimuli.csv`). Through C03, its p10/p90 set the operating point at which ∂Flex/∂T_out is evaluated; its σ widens the RD spread across scenarios. Shadow price: ∂Flex↑/∂T_out — colder outdoor temperature increases heating demand and changes the heat-pump headroom.

---

## Validity Limits
- Gaussian errors: adequate for 2 m temperature at hourly to day-ahead leads; not for extremes (heatwaves, cold snaps), which belong to W-series extreme models.
- Needs 14 days of local observations for T_hist; before that, b2 = 0 and b1 = 1 (pure NWP) with a flag.
- Coefficients are site-specific; a site-level bias correction (W17, maritime +0.5 °C typical) must not be applied on top of a trained W02 (it would double-correct).
- D01 range check T_out ∈ [−15, 45] °C; rate-of-change ≤ 5 °C per 15 min.
