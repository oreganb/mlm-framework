# M09 — Carbon Price Model

## Purpose
Carbon Price Model in the [[M-Series]]. Layer: **Stochastic**.

## Formula

### LaTeX
$$C_{carbon}(t) = P_{ETS}(t) \cdot E_{emission}(t)$$

### Description
Carbon cost from EU ETS allowance price applied to net grid emissions. ETS price forecast via ARIMA on historical EU ETS spot price.

### Method
EU ETS price forecast, ARIMA

---

## Inputs

| Symbol | Description | Unit | Source Model/Dataset | Source Field | Weight | Weight Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P_ETS |  | €/tCO₂ | DS-CARBON | `ets_price_eur_t` | 1.0 | ETS allowance price |
| E_emission |  | tCO₂ | K01 | `emissions_kg` | 1/1000 | convert kg to t |

---

## Outputs

| Symbol | Description | Unit | Consumed By |
|---|---|---|---|
| C_carbon | Carbon cost of net grid emissions | € | [[K03]] (ETS liability reporting) |
| P_ETS[k] | EU ETS allowance price forecast per step (median and quantiles) | EUR/tCO₂ | [[C03]] → [[C01]] as `p_CO2[k]` = `P_ETS[k]` / 1000 EUR/kgCO₂ (KG-CR-07; C01 owner decision of 2026-10-23) |

## AI Context (Cursor Alignment)
ETS price is a policy input. Its forecast `P_ETS` (EUR/tCO₂) is an output: [[C01]] prices carbon in its objective at `p_CO2[k]` = `P_ETS[k]` / 1000 EUR/kgCO₂ per step, passed through [[C03]] at the median (owner decision of 2026-10-23; MLM SPECS2 KG-CR-07). It also informs K03 compliance reporting and longer-term investment decisions.
